## The headline finding

- **86.6% of unfilled volume was held in another depot the same week**
- 97.5% of unfilled *events* were coverable the same way
- Depot-SKU-weeks under-supplied: 14.1%; over-supplied: 12.2%
- Receipts ran at 92.4% of plan
- Network weeks of cover: 1.96 (target 2.1)

Test is strict: same SKU, same ISO week, not "some time that month".

## Suppliers

Cheapest supplier is the most damaging in all three material categories tested:

| Material | Supplier | Price ₹/kg | True cost ₹/kg | Production lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ADDITIVE | Crest Performance Chem | 248.70 | 328.10 | 9,986.50 kL |
| BASE_OIL_G3 | Arabian Synthetics | 120.80 | 181.90 | 7,334.70 kL |
| BASE_OIL_G2 | Gulf Star Base Oils | 97.40 | 127.00 | 4,347.50 kL |

True cost = purchase spend + gross margin lost downstream, per kg received.

Import exposure: 46.2% of spend, 100% of Group III. Actual lead time 38.9 days
against 34.8 contracted. Nine of the ten least reliable suppliers are imported
(the exception is Vikram Additive Works, domestic).

Bias vs variance is the distinction to draw: bias is plannable by changing the
contract lead time, variance is not and must be bought off with safety stock.

## Tail test — hypothesis tested and REJECTED

| Metric | A-class | C-class |
|---|---|---|
| Share of SKUs | — | 39.2% |
| Share of volume | — | 5.1% |
| Margin per ₹1 of stock per year | ₹14.2 | ₹9.0 |
| OTIF | 86.18% | 87.68% |

C-class is less productive than A but comfortably profitable, and its service is
slightly *better*. Do not recommend delisting. The real tail cost is the minimum
stocking lot per depot — a stocking-policy issue, not a range issue.

## Capacity

- Plant runs at 79–85% of 159,000 kL nameplate
- Nameplate breached at 1.22× current demand
- Gurugram over weekly throughput capacity in 64 of 156 weeks
- Bhiwandi and Chennai 47 weeks each; Kolkata 0

## Seasonality

- Monsoon OTIF 85.54% vs 86.55% non-monsoon — a small gap
- In-full is **higher** in monsoon, because demand falls ~19% and the same stock
  covers more of it
- Worst month is **October at 82.87%** — demand snaps back while replenishment,
  set from the prior week, is still sized for the low period. Best single piece
  of evidence that the planning lag is the mechanism.

## Calibration anchor

Plant sized on ExxonMobil's lubricant plant at the Isambe Industrial Area,
Raigad, Maharashtra: ~₹900 crore (USD $110m), 159,000 kilolitres of finished
Mobil lubricants per year, announced March 2023, start-up targeted end-2025.
Source: ExxonMobil corporate newsroom.

## Known data-quality artefacts

- 24,175 order lines (6.9%) have NULL delivery date — credit holds and total
  stockouts. Correct, not missing data.
- 4 rows where `fail_reason` contradicts `in_full_flag`, within a 0.5 L rounding
  tolerance.
- 2 RAW_MATERIAL_SHORT batches have no `causing_po_id`.
- `is_emergency_transfer` is set on 3,016 lines but only 487 shipped.
- `tier_code` and `base_oil_group` are perfectly correlated by design.
